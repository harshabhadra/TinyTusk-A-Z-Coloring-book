    About TinyTusk Books

    TinyTusk Books makes gentle first learning books for little hands. Big friendly shapes, clear steps and one small idea per page, so kids can sit down, pick up a crayon or pencil and feel proud of what they made.

    Questions parents ask

    Which book should we start with?

    For ages 3 to 5 learning letters, start with TinyTusk ABC Letter Tracing Workbook, then Trace & Write the Alphabet for more practice with words and sentences. For coloring fun, A to Z Animals Copy Coloring Book suits ages 3 to 8 and the Color by Number book ages 4 to 8. Compare the books.

    What is the TinyTusk Learning Series?

    Our three newest books: TinyTusk A to Z Animals Color by Number, TinyTusk ABC Letter Tracing Workbook and Trace & Write the Alphabet. They share the same look and the same animal friends as our first book, A to Z Animals Copy Coloring Book.

    What does “copy coloring” mean?

    In A to Z Animals Copy Coloring Book, each page has a small finished picture in color. Your child copies those colors onto the big outline, using the “Check your colors” dots as a guide.

    Crayons, pencils or markers?

    Crayons and colored pencils work best for the coloring books, and a regular pencil for the tracing workbooks. If you use markers, slip a sheet of card behind the page so ink doesn't reach the next one.

    How big are the books?

    All four are 8.5 × 11 inch paperbacks. A to Z Animals Copy Coloring Book has 30 pages, Color by Number 40, ABC Letter Tracing Workbook 62 and Trace & Write the Alphabet 82.

    When and where can I buy them?

    All four are paperbacks on Amazon. A to Z Animals Copy Coloring Book is in final review now, and the three Learning Series books are coming soon. Each book above shows a Buy on Amazon button as soon as it's live. Leave your email to get the links.

    Can I print the free pages more than once?

    Yes, print them as often as you like for your family or classroom.
