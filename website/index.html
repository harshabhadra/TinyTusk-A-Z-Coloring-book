    About us

    TinyTusk makes gentle first activity books for little hands. Big friendly shapes, bright clear colors and one small idea per page, so kids can sit down, pick up a crayon and feel proud of what they made.

    Questions parents ask

    What age is it for?

    Ages 3 to 8. Younger kids enjoy the big outlines and the animals; older kids practise matching colors and learning letter sounds.

    What does “copy coloring” mean?

    Each page has a small finished picture in color. Your child copies those colors onto the big outline, using the “Check your colors” dots as a guide.

    Crayons, pencils or markers?

    Crayons and colored pencils work best. If you use markers, slip a sheet of card behind the page so ink doesn't reach the next picture.

    How big is the book?

    8.5 × 11 inches (US Letter), 30 pages, including a title page, a name page, 26 letter pages and a “Well done!” certificate.
