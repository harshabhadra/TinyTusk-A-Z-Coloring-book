## Text for the modules

The images already carry their message, so keep the module text short. A+ also allows modules with **no** text; leave the text fields empty if you prefer the images alone.

**Module 2: header**
- Headline: `Learn the alphabet the fun way`
- Body: `Meet 26 cute baby animals, from a smiling Alligator to a stripy Zebra. Each page pairs a big outline picture with a small colored picture to copy, so little ones can color, copy and learn every letter.`

**Module 3: how it works**
- Headline: `Three simple steps on every page`
- Body: `Color the big hollow letters, copy the small colored picture, and use the "check your colors" dots to find the right crayons.`

**Module 4: color and copy**
- Headline: `A picture to color and a picture to copy`
- Body: `Big shapes and thick lines are easy for little hands. The colored picture shows one way to color it, and children can always choose their own colors.`

**Module 5: all 26 animals**
- Headline: `One baby animal for every letter`
- Body: `From familiar friends like the Lion and Panda to new ones like the Narwhal, Urial and X-ray fish.`

**Module 6: three tiles**
- 1: `Big shapes, thick lines` / `Large areas that are easy to color for ages 3 to 8.`
- 2: `Copy the colored picture` / `A small color guide on every page.`
- 3: `Learn every letter` / `Big hollow upper and lower case letters to color.`

**Module 7: what's inside**
- Headline: `What's inside`
- Body: `A title page, a "This book belongs to" page, 26 letter pages and a "Well done!" certificate to celebrate finishing the book.`

## A+ rules (why the images look the way they do)
A+ Content can be rejected for: prices or discounts, "buy now" or other calls to action, customer reviews or star ratings, words like "best" or "#1", time-sensitive words ("new", "now available"), links, emails or contact details, and shipping or guarantee claims. None of these appear in the images or the text above; keep it that way if you edit them.
