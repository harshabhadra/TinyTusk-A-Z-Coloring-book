## Two-week posting plan

| Day | Post | Platform |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch post + story with link sticker | Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp status |
| 2 | Reel A to Z | Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts |
| 3 | Pinterest pin | Pinterest (pin it to 2–3 boards) |
| 4 | Carousel (all 5 slides) | Instagram, Facebook |
| 6 | Free coloring page offer | Instagram, Facebook parent groups (check each group's rules on promotion) |
| 8 | Story: "Meet all 26" slide + poll | Instagram story |
| 10 | Reel again with a different song, or a real photo/video of a child coloring a page (with parent permission) | Reels, TikTok |
| 12 | Carousel slide 3 ("Color it… like this!") as a single post | Instagram, Facebook |
| 14 | Ask buyers for an honest Amazon review in a story; repost any colored pages people tag you in | Instagram |

**Tips**
- **Show real coloring:** once you get your author copy, a short video of real crayons on a real page gets the most engagement.
- **Don't buy or swap reviews:** Amazon removes them and can penalize the book. Asking happy buyers for an honest review is fine.
- **Keep the TinyTusk name consistent** in every bio and handle (e.g. @tinytuskbooks) so people can find you.
